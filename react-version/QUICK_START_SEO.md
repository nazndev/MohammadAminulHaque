## 🚀 Immediate Actions (This Week)

### 1. Set Up Google Search Console (FREE - Do This First!)
**Why**: This is Google's official tool to see how your site performs in search.

**Steps**:
1. Go to [search.google.com/search-console](https://search.google.com/search-console)
2. Click "Add Property"
3. Enter: `mohammadaminulhaque.net`
4. Verify ownership (DNS or HTML file method)
5. Submit sitemap: `https://mohammadaminulhaque.net/sitemap.xml`
6. Check "Performance" tab daily to see:
   - What keywords people search
   - Your current rankings
   - Click-through rates
   - Impressions

**Time**: 30 minutes | **Cost**: FREE

### 2. Set Up Google Analytics (FREE)
**Why**: Track who visits your site and how they find it.

**Steps**:
1. Go to [analytics.google.com](https://analytics.google.com)
2. Create account
3. Add tracking code to your website (in `app/layout.tsx`)
4. Monitor traffic sources

**Time**: 20 minutes | **Cost**: FREE

### 3. Submit to Google News Publisher Center
**Why**: Get your news articles indexed in Google News.

**Steps**:
1. Go to [publishercenter.google.com](https://publishercenter.google.com)
2. Create account
3. Add your website
4. Verify ownership
5. Submit RSS feed or sitemap

**Time**: 1 hour | **Cost**: FREE
